Registration Help

How to Create Your Account:

  1. Fill in your personal information - Use your real name as it appears on official documents
  2. Enter a valid phone number with country code (+256 for Uganda) - We'll use this for important SMS notifications
  3. Provide a valid email address for account recovery and system notifications
  4. Create a secure password with at least 8 characters
  5. Confirm your password by typing it again

Why do we need this information?

  • Phone number - Used for SMS alerts about student attendance, fees, and emergencies
  • Email - Used for password recovery and formal communications
  • Name - Required for official reports and certificates
