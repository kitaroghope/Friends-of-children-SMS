School Registration Help

How to Register Your School:

  1. Requester Information - Fill in your personal details. You will be the primary administrator for this school's account.
  2. School Information - Provide official details about your school including name, contact info, and address.
  3. Submit Request - Our team will review your registration within 2-3 business days.

Field Explanations:

  • Acronym - A short form for your school used throughout the system (e.g., "BFA" for "Best Friends Academy")
  • Currency - This sets the default currency for all fee collections and financial reports
  • Additional Notes - Tell us about your school size, programs, or special requirements

After Approval:

Once approved, you'll receive login credentials via email to complete your school setup and invite staff members.
