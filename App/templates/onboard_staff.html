Staff Onboarding Help

How to Add a Staff Member:

  1. Enter your JWT token for authentication
  2. Fill in account information (email and password for login)
  3. Provide personal information about the staff member
  4. Enter employment details (staff number, department, position)
  5. Assign roles for system access permissions
  6. Click Add Staff Member to save

Field Explanations:

  • Staff Number - Unique identifier for payroll and records
  • Department - Academic, Administration, Finance, etc.
  • Position - Teacher, Head of Department, Secretary, etc.
  • Roles - Control what the staff member can access in the system

Common Role Codes:

  • academic.view_results - View student results
  • academic.enter_marks - Enter exam marks
  • academic.approve_results - Approve results for publication
  • finance.view_invoices - View fee invoices
  • students.view - View student information

After Adding:

The staff member will receive login credentials and can access the system based on their assigned roles.
